What is Phishing?

A cyber attack where criminals impersonate trusted brands to steal your passwords, money, or identity.

Email Phishing

Fake emails that look like they're from your bank, PayPal, or employer — with a malicious link or attachment.

Website Spoofing

Fake login pages that mimic real ones (paypal-secure.xyz). You type your password — they steal it.

Smishing / Vishing

Phishing via SMS (smishing) or voice calls (vishing) — asking you to "verify" your account.
